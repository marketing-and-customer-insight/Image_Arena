# images/real/ — real advertisement benchmark set

These 45 files are **third-party display-advertisement creatives** used as a
research benchmark in an academic study (Universität Hamburg, Marketing &
Customer Insight). They are hosted here solely so that a survey platform can
render them to study participants alongside AI-generated advertising images.

- **What they are:** real online display ads (Google display-network
  inventory), collected from live ad placements in 2026. One ad per study
  prompt, selected by semantic similarity to 45 brand-manager briefs.
- **Unmodified:** the files are exactly as collected — no cropping, no
  recomposition, no retouching. Filenames (`P###_n.jpeg`) index them to the
  study's prompt IDs and carry no other meaning.
- **Not an endorsement:** the brands shown have no involvement in this
  research. Trademarks and creative content remain the property of their
  respective owners. The files are reproduced here under fair-dealing /
  research-use considerations, for non-commercial scientific purposes only.
- **Removal requests:** if you are a rights holder and want a file removed,
  contact **claus.hegmann@uni-hamburg.de** — it will be taken down promptly.
